SHOOTING

Meter ON: hold S and release in the green at the top of the bar. Yellow = slightly early or late, red = way off.
Meter OFF: release when the shooter's wrist snaps at the top of his jump. Same green/yellow/red timing as 2K's no-meter mode.
Green window grows with your shooting rating and badges (Deadeye, Catch & Shoot) and shrinks when a defender contests.
